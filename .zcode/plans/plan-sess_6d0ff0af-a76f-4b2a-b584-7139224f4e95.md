## Step 5 — `analysis.py`
- `find_equilibrium_position(potential_fn, center_guess, waist)` — bounded, dimensionless scalar (Brent/`brentq` on force sign around center, or minimize potential in units of `w`); returns `(x_eq, U_eq, |F_residual|)`.
- `numerical_curvature(force_fn or potential_fn, x0, h, waist)` — central second difference; call at `h` and `h/2` (h = `curvature_step_waist*waist`); return both + convergence diff `|κ_h − κ_{h/2}|`.
- `numerical_trap_frequency(kappa, mass)`, `trap_frequency_analytic` re-export.
- `estimate_frequency_from_trajectory(times, displacement, method="interpolated_zero_crossings", min_cycles)` — linear-interpolated zero crossings of displacement-from-mean; period = 2·mean spacing; require ≥ `min_cycles`. Also return FFT diagnostic: peak bin freq + raw resolution `1/T`.
- `estimate_nonlinear_frequency_by_quadrature(depth, waist, center, mass, amplitude)` — period T = 4∫₀^A du / √(2(E−U(xc+u))/m), E=U(turning); dimensionless `s=u/w`, `scipy.integrate.quad` with splitting at the turning point to handle the integrable singularity; returns f=1/T. (Verified in harmonic limit → f₀.)
- `calculate_relative_error(value, reference, zero_reference_action="return_nan")` — explicit zero-handling.
- `summarize_static_trap(...)` — assemble per-trap metrics dict (see Step 8 schema), separating **discretization error** (`|f_num_curv − f_analytic|/f_analytic`, target `< curvature_relative_tolerance`) from **anharmonic shift** (`(f_small − f_nonlinear)/f_small`, reported, ~1%) from **trajectory-vs-nonlinear** (`|f_traj − f_nonlinear|/f_nonlinear`, tight).
